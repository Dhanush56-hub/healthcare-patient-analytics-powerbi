# Healthcare Patient Analytics — Project Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes 10,000 healthcare patient records using Power BI.

The dashboard focuses on patient volume, treatment costs, department performance, length of stay, patient satisfaction, outcomes, and readmissions.

---

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Patients | 10,000 |
| Total Treatment Cost | ₹25.34 Crore |
| Average Treatment Cost | ₹25.34K |
| Average Length of Stay | 3.73 Days |
| Readmission Rate | 13.27% |

---

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Highest Patient Volume — General Medicine

General Medicine recorded the highest patient volume with 2,055 patients.

This indicates that General Medicine may require careful monitoring of staffing, resources, and capacity.

### 2. Highest Treatment Cost — Oncology

Oncology recorded the highest total treatment cost of approximately ₹8.59 crore.

This makes Oncology the largest contributor to overall treatment expenditure.

### 3. Highest Length of Stay — Oncology

Oncology had the highest average length of stay at 7.13 days.

The longer stay may indicate greater treatment complexity and higher resource requirements.

### 4. Highest Readmission Rate — Oncology

Oncology recorded the highest readmission rate at 18.18%, compared with the overall hospital readmission rate of 13.27%.

This makes Oncology an important area for further investigation.

### 5. Highest Patient Satisfaction — Neurology

Neurology recorded the highest average patient satisfaction score of 3.98 out of 5.

Practices contributing to this higher satisfaction could be studied and applied to other departments where appropriate.

---

## Key Finding

The strongest pattern identified in the analysis was:

**Oncology had the highest treatment cost, highest average length of stay, and highest readmission rate.**

This suggests that Oncology should be investigated further to understand treatment complexity, resource utilization, discharge planning, follow-up, and factors contributing to readmissions.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Review staffing and resource allocation in high-volume departments.
- Investigate major treatment cost drivers within Oncology.
- Analyze the reasons for longer hospital stays.
- Improve discharge planning where appropriate.
- Investigate factors contributing to Oncology readmissions.
- Strengthen post-treatment follow-up where appropriate.
- Study practices associated with higher patient satisfaction.

## Data Analysis Process

**Raw Data → Data Cleaning → Data Transformation → Data Modeling → DAX → Visualization → Business Insights**

Power Query was used for data cleaning and transformation.

DAX was used to create KPIs and analytical measures.

Power BI was used to build interactive dashboards, slicers, charts, and patient-level drill-through analysis.
